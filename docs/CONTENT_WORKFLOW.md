# Content Workflow

## Weekly Workflow
1. Open AI小圆桌.
2. Choose or confirm the current week range.
3. Trigger RSS fetch.
4. Review candidate hotspots.
5. Manually add any important missing hotspot.
6. Select one hotspot.
7. Generate a central-agent roundtable plan.
8. Review role setup, agenda, tensions, and source risks.
9. Generate the roundtable draft.
10. Edit for clarity, factual caution, and differentiated viewpoints.
11. Save as `draft`, then mark `reviewed` or `published` when ready.

## RSS Sources
Default source categories:
- AI company blogs.
- Research feeds.
- Developer ecosystem feeds.
- Market and business sources.
- Policy and regulation sources.
- Other curated sources.

The app should show source name, category, enabled state, last fetch status, and last fetch time.

## Candidate Rules
MVP ranking can stay simple:
- Published within the selected week.
- AI-related keywords in title or summary.
- Source category is trusted.
- Multiple sources point to the same theme.
- Model release, research progress, product update, funding/business event, open-source project, or regulation event.

## Review Rules
Before saving or publishing:
- Source links are present.
- Simulated role nature is clear.
- No unsupported numbers, dates, funding amounts, or company claims.
- Technical and investor perspectives add different value.
- The final takeaways are useful for AI practitioners.
